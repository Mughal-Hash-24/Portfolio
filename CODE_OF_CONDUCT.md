# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its visitors.
- Credit others for their work and ideas.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberate bad-faith contributions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, and issues that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.

Report issues to **ibtasaam.mughal@gmail.com**. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
